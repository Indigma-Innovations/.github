## Welcome to Indigma on GitHub 👋

At Indigma, we engineer Responsible AI Solutions that prioritize privacy, transparency, sustainability, and ethical innovation across software and AI systems. Our work spans machine learning, federated learning, generative AI, blockchain technologies, and enterprise applications, all designed to empower businesses and research initiatives with cutting-edge, responsible technology.

### 🚀 About Us
Indigma is a team dedicated to building AI and software solutions rooted in responsible innovation. We believe that technology should be secure, explainable, and impactful, helping enterprises and startups navigate the future with integrity and trust.

#### What We Do

Our areas of expertise include:
- **Federated Learning**: privacy-preserving AI systems
- **Generative & Agentic AI**: advanced automated decision-making systems
- **Blockchain-integrated Solutions**: for transparency and data integrity
- **User-centric Software Development**: delivering full-stack application solutions

### 📁 Repository Organization

This GitHub organization hosts our publicly visible source projects, although the majority of our active development happens across 20 private repositories that are accessible only to our core team.

Together, these repositories power the workflows, services, and research prototypes that embody Indigma’s mission to responsibly innovate in AI and software engineering.
